How to Play

  • Join with a room code (or scan the lobby QR) — no account needed. Playing alone? Hit Quick Play vs Bot, or add a 🤖 computer player of any level: 🍬 Rookie, ⚔️ Veteran, 🔥 Elite or 👑 Legend.
  • Each question gives everyone 15 seconds. Tap an answer and it locks in instantly. Faster and correct = more points: 200–1000 per question. Wrong or no answer = 0.
  • 🔥 Combos — answer correctly in a row and every extra streak step adds bonus points (up to +200).
  • ✂️ 50/50 (2 per game) removes two wrong answers for 60% points. 📊 Audience Poll (1 per game) shows what the studio audience thinks — usually right, not always — for 80% points.
  • 🗺️ Levels & the map — a match is 2–4 levels (Qualifier → … → Grand Final). Each level is a room, and every player sits at their own table as an animated character. Lamps and numbers show who locked in first and how fast. Tap the map to zoom out and see every room.
  • ☠️ Elimination — at the end of each level the storm closes on the room: the lowest scorers are knocked out (with 2 players left, nobody is cut). The survivors stand up, run down the corridor and sit at new tables in the next room. Out? Stay and watch the rest of the tournament from the stands.
  • ← The back arrow (or your phone's back gesture) lets you leave a room or match at any time.
  • 🤝 Team mode — the host can split the room into 2–4 colour teams. Scores are shared; with 3+ teams the lowest team is knocked out together at the end of each level.
  • 📅 Daily Challenge — the same 12 questions for everyone each day (India time). Your best score of the day is shown on the button, and the Hall of Fame has a “Today” filter.
  • 😎 Reactions pop up over your table for everyone. If you're eliminated, tap a contender's table to cheer for them. The computer players talk back, too. 🎙️ turns on a spoken voice host.
  • 🌆 City Mission — an open-world city with wandering pedestrians and traffic. Walk (joystick / WASD / tap) to the marked building on the map; at the door a quiz gate opens and only a correct answer unlocks the reward: 🔫 a blaster that stuns rivals for 3 s, 🚗 a turbo car, 🛡️ a shield or 💰 cash. Wrong answer = locked out for a few seconds. Finish all missions and reach the ✈️ airport first to win.
  • 🏅 At the end you get awards — Fastest Finger, Streak King, Sharpshooter, Point Thief, Comeback Kid.
  • 🇮🇳 Try the Hinglish GK category, or in Study Mode pick English / Hinglish / Hindi and even paste your own notes or upload a PDF to turn them into a quiz.
  • Every 3rd question is a power round, alternating between:
  • ⚡ Steal Round — the fastest correct answer can steal 150 points from one opponent.
  • 🥶 Freeze Round — the fastest correct answer can freeze one opponent out of the very next question.
  • After the last question, whoever has the most points wins. Play Again reuses the same room and code.
  • 📝 Custom / Study Mode — build your own quiz: pick a level (Class 1 to PhD or a competitive exam), a category and sub-category, and let AI write the questions — or add your own by hand.
  • 🎨 Tap the palette to switch between Candy Blast, Battle Zone, Neon City, Midnight and Daylight.
