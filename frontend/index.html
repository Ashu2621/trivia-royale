How to Play

  • 🌆 The goal — you are loose in a 3D city with your friends (or 🤖 computer rivals). Your cash is your score. Whoever has the most when the 8 minutes are up wins — and finishing all four missions earns a big bonus.
  • 🎯 Missions — 1) 🔫 the Gun Store (grab guns), 2) 🏚️ the Haunted Mansion, 3) 💰 the Bank heist, 4) 🚽 the Royal Restroom (finish). Stand in the glowing ring at a door and hold still to complete it. Side stops work any time: 🏥 Hospital heals, 🚗 Garage gives a car, 🍔 Diner, 🎮 Arcade and 📻 Radio pay cash, 🚓 Police lets you bribe your stars away.
  • 🥊 Fighting — Space / J or a mouse click / tap / the 👊 button attacks (hold to keep going; it auto-aims at whoever is in front of you). Fists, 🏏 bat, 🔫 pistol, SMG and shotgun (1–5 or Tab to switch). Armed guards protect every building, and other players will fight back — or take revenge.
  • 🚗 Cars — walk up to a car and press F (or 🚗) to hijack it. Drive with WASD (or the stick). Run people over, but cars crash, catch fire and explode.
  • ⭐ Wanted level — hurt people, rob the bank or blow things up and the cops come, more of them with every star. Lose them by hiding, waiting it out, or bribing the police.
  • 💀 Wasted? You respawn at the hospital after a few seconds with your pistol (half the ammo) and a moment of protection. You drop 20% of your cash on the street (grab it back!) and whoever took you out earns +$250.
  • 🚽 The bladder — everyone's bladder keeps filling. Your character starts dancing, holding it with both hands and letting out toots. Find a blue public toilet stall on a street corner (hold the door) — or reach the mansion and Royal Restroom. At 100% you have an accident: −$250 and everyone hears it.
  • 🏚️ The Haunted Mansion — a maze with 3 keys 🔑 (just walk into them for +$300) that open lettered gates, ghosts 👻 that add to your bladder and freeze you, and a 🚽 exit worth $1000 with instant relief. F = flashlight stuns nearby ghosts.
  • 🎙️ Microphone — tap the mic to talk with your whole gang using real voice chat. Shout into it and nearby ghosts flee (and your character screams).
  • ← The back arrow (or your phone's back gesture) lets you leave a room or match at any time.
  • 🏅 At the end there are awards — Top Gun, Mission Master, Punching Bag, Leaky Pants / Iron Bladder and Loudest Cheeks — and your score goes to the 🏆 Hall of Fame.
